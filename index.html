Explain Fourier Series & Dirichlet Conditions

Verified against University Marking Rubric

📌 Core Concept

A Fourier Series decomposes any periodic function $f(x)$ with period $2L$ into an infinite sum of simple sine and cosine harmonics:

f(x) = (a₀ / 2) + Σ [ aₙ cos(nπx / L) + bₙ sin(nπx / L) ]

🔑 Dirichlet Conditions (Must memorize for 5-Mark Questions)

  • 1. Single-valued & periodic: $f(x)$ is single-valued and periodic within its fundamental interval.
  • 2. Finite extrema: $f(x)$ has only a finite number of maxima and minima within one period.
  • 3. Finite discontinuities: $f(x)$ has only a finite number of finite jump discontinuities.
💡 University Exam Tip: If $f(x)$ is an even function ($f(-x) = f(x)$), then $b_n = 0$! Only compute $a_0$ and $a_n$ to save 15 minutes during your exam paper.
